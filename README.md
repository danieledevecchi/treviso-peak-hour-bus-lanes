# Evaluation of Peak-Hour Bus Lane Implementation on the Treviso Urban Ring

## Project Overview

This independent transport engineering study assesses the feasibility of introducing dedicated bus lanes during peak hours along Treviso's urban ring road. It investigates existing traffic conditions, public transport operations and infrastructure constraints, proposing a reallocation of road space to improve bus reliability while integrating cycling infrastructure.

## Methodology and Tools

- **QGIS:** Spatial analysis and mapping of transport networks, road layouts and accident hotspots.
- **AutoCAD:** Preliminary redesign of a key intersection to accommodate dedicated bus lanes and cycling infrastructure.
- **Operational analysis:** Scenario-based assessment of potential bus travel-time improvements and operational savings.

## Key Results

The proposed intervention could generate an estimated 295 annual bus operating hours saved, based on indicative travel-time scenarios and existing service frequencies.

The study also examines potential implications for traffic redistribution, public transport attractiveness and transport-related emissions.

## Scope and Limitations

This is a preliminary feasibility assessment based on available data and explicit operational assumptions. Detailed traffic microsimulation, field validation and a comprehensive cost-benefit analysis would be required before implementation.
